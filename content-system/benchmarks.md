- Waitlist-to-customer conversion > 20% = strong product-market fit; best launches hit 40-50%
- Pre-launch best practices we follow: hints/narrative without premature reveals, referral loops (we have referral codes built), urgency beats in the final phase, launch-day email/social sequence
