# FORTREX CROWN SYSTEM — the visual design operating system
> LOCKED Sep 26, 2026 (founder: "that is the direction I'd lock"). This is the master visual canon. It extends the live design canon (obsidian #050506, gold #D8A64D, bone #FFF7E6, graphite #111114, Space Grotesk / Inter / JetBrains Mono — confirmed unchanged). DESIGN-LANGUAGE.md holds the pixel-measured crown DNA; this doc is the application system across every surface. Precedence: this doc + live canon. Any future design work is checked against it. A person must recognize FORTREX even when the logo is absent.

## 0. THE PRINCIPLE
The brand communicates: POWER + PRECISION + COMPETITION + STATUS + CONTROL + TRUST.
NOT luxury + gold + random 3D effects.
The style is already inside the logo. Never invent a separate "futuristic style". Extract, don't decorate.

## 1. GEOMETRY (extracted from the crown)
Sharp peaks, shields, diamonds, faceted polygons, vertical symmetry, angular cuts, layered structures, pointed silhouettes, architectural frames.
Applied SUBTLY to: cards (tiny crown-inspired top geometry, gold hairline, angular corner detail), buttons, dividers, backgrounds, icons, charts, transitions, video frames, image crops, section shapes.
The user feels the crown without seeing the crown repeatedly.

## 2. COLOR (90% dark/neutral, 10% gold signal)
- Obsidian #050506 (environment) · Deep black #000000 (depth)
- FORTREX Gold #D8A64D (accent ONLY)
- Bone #FFF7E6 (light/text)
- Support: charcoal, graphite, gunmetal, muted bronze, smoke gray
GOLD IS AN ACCENT, NOT A BACKGROUND. Gold means IMPORTANT: verified, winner, rank, action, championship. If everything is gold, the brand is cheap. This is the most important rule in the system.

## 3. MATERIALS
Obsidian + black metal + polished gold + glass + faceted crystal.
Website: dark architectural surfaces. Dashboard: black glass/gunmetal panels. Visuals: sharp metallic edges. Video: controlled reflective highlights. Photography: hard directional key + soft atmospheric fill. 3D: faceted surfaces.
BANNED: plastic, cartoon gradients, cheap chrome, rainbow neon, generic cyberpunk, excessive glow, gaming RGB.

## 4. LIGHTING (the crown works because of light on edges/facets)
Sequence: BLACK → FORM → EDGE LIGHT → GOLD DETAIL → REVEAL.
Dark environment, controlled highlight, gold edge, deep shadow. Defines: hero lighting, 3D renders, video intros, winner announcements, tournament reveals, social graphics, product photography.

## 5. BACKGROUND FAMILY (never random backgrounds)
A Obsidian void · B Faceted architecture (subtle crown-derived polygons) · C Metallic surface (dark brushed) · D Glass chamber · E Tournament arena (dark architecture + gold structural lines) · F Data grid (very subtle technical grid).

## 6. TYPOGRAPHY (financial institution, not gaming company)
- Space Grotesk: headlines, major numbers, tournament names, hero
- Inter: body, navigation, descriptions, forms
- JetBrains Mono: trader IDs, scores, timestamps, rank, trade data, system info
Feels: precise + engineered + expensive. Extremely clean spacing; numbers are a brand asset.

## 7. LOGO SYSTEM (never a giant logo everywhere)
- Full crown: website hero, launch, major announcements, championships, winner content, brand films
- Crown mark: favicon, avatar, app icon, verified badge, small UI
- Crown + wordmark: official comms, header, social profiles, documents, partnerships
- Crown watermark (very light): video, winner cards, premium photography, backgrounds

## 8. UI LANGUAGE — the platform feels like the inside of the crown
Dark, structured, precise. Gold highlights only important states (VERIFIED, #01, ACTIVE).
Cards: NOT everything is a floating glass card. Hierarchy: surface → line → information → accent. Some elements sit directly on the background.
Buttons: primary = dark/gold interaction; secondary = bone/graphite; danger = red only when genuinely necessary. Controls, not colorful SaaS widgets.
Icons: one geometric language, thin-to-medium line weight, angular. No mixed icon sets.
Charts: black background, thin grid, GOLD PRIMARY DATA ONLY, bone labels, muted comparison lines. Gold = focus, never every series.

## 9. SHIELD = trust language / CROWN = status language / FACETS = data language
- Shield (geometric, never emoji): VERIFIED, OFFICIAL TOURNAMENT, FOUNDING MEMBER — protection, verification, trust.
- Crown geometry behind numbers: RANK #017, DIVISION ELITE, champion/prestige/genesis/Hall of Fame.
- Facets become: charts, score breakdowns, rank transitions — sharp geometry, thin gold lines, dark surfaces, high contrast.

## 10. MOTION SYSTEM (Crown Motion — no generic fade/bounce/particles)
- Logo reveal: black → tiny gold edge → facets illuminate → shield forms → crown expands → FORTREX
- Tournament start: dark → geometry activates → countdown → gold line sweep → ARENA OPEN
- Verification: data connects → facets lock → shield closes → VERIFIED
- Winner reveal: black → rank → gold light → facets → CROWN → WINNER
These are recognizable FORTREX signatures.

## 11. VIDEO LANGUAGE
Intro: 0-1s black · 1-2s tiny gold edge · 2-4s crown geometry assembles · 4-5s wordmark · content. Short enough to become recognizable without the name.
Transitions: geometric cuts, shield wipes, gold-line sweeps, facet reveals, black-frame transitions, controlled camera push, architectural reveals. BANNED: cartoon wipes, rainbow transitions, glitch overload, sparks, gaming effects.

## 12. PHOTOGRAPHY
Cinematic + premium + controlled + competitive. Dark environment, single strong directional key, gold rim, soft fill. Central symmetry, negative space, architectural backgrounds, dark wardrobe, gold detail.
TRADERS: quiet confidence — dark room, laptop glow, gold edge light, face partially lit, data reflected. NO Lamborghini, no fake money, no millionaire lifestyle, no motivational clichés (they destroy the brand).
WINNERS: championship photography, not "congrats bro 🔥" — season, rank, name [verified], final score, crown.

## 13. TOURNAMENT IDENTITY
Every tournament gets identity inside the universe: FORTREX // S01, GENESIS 01 — slightly different geometric motifs, clearly FORTREX. Championship moments: MORE negative space, bigger crown, stronger lighting, MORE gold, SLOWER motion, LESS information (hierarchy).

## 14. SOCIAL + COMMUNITY
Template family (all visibly FORTREX): tournament announcement, countdown, registration open, leaderboard, rank change, winner, top 10, record, verified trader, season, championship.
Broadcast cards, not casual startup posts: "FORTREX // LIVE · #127 JUST MOVED TO #08 · +19 POSITIONS".
UGC ENGINE (huge): auto-generated branded share cards for achievements — MY FIRST WIN, TOP 1%, RANK #27, SEASON FINALIST, GENESIS MEMBER. Free advertising generated by users.
Profile asset system: PFP, banner, highlights, thumbnails, headers, Discord/Telegram, creator kit — same black+gold+crown geometry.

## 15. CONTENT LANGUAGE (verbal = visual: calm, precise, powerful)
Short, confident, declarative, institutional. THE ARENA OPENS. 10,000 TRADERS. ONE BOARD. PROVE YOUR EDGE. NEVER: 🚀🔥 HUGE TOURNAMENT!!!
Official format: FORTREX // ANNOUNCEMENT · SEASON ZERO · NOVEMBER 7 · REGISTRATION OPENS 09:00 IST.

## 16. AUDIO IDENTITY (the forgotten dimension)
Logo sound, tournament start, verification, rank-up, winner reveal, notification, championship. Deep, metallic, precise, minimal, cinematic — NOT EDM gamer noise. Palette: low bass, deep drones, subtle metallic impacts, slow cinematic pulse, minimal electronic texture; intensity differs for brand/competition/winner/announcement/community.

## 17. 3D ASSET LIBRARY
Reusable crown-derived objects: crown, shield, facet, gold edge, fortress, diamond, trophy, rank emblem, verification shield, REX emblem. Every video/image/website section uses the same assets.

## 18. AI GENERATION STANDARDS (append to every prompt)
Base prompt: "FORTREX visual language: obsidian-black architectural environment, sharp faceted geometry derived from the FORTREX crown silhouette, controlled metallic gold edge lighting, deep shadows, premium institutional aesthetic, symmetrical composition, cinematic contrast, engineered precision, restrained atmosphere, no neon, no generic fintech stock aesthetic, no excessive glow, no text unless explicitly specified." + the actual scene. Same universe, different story.
Video prompts additionally carry: FORTREX lighting/materials/geometry/camera/motion/sound/emotional tone.
Design/coding agents: never invent another design language; every new page inherits tokens, components, spacing, typography, colors, motion, geometry, states.

## 19. THE ONE-PAGE DNA (controls everything)
Shape: crown/shield/facet · Material: obsidian/metal/glass · Accent: gold · Light: edge lighting · Composition: symmetry · Typography: geometric+technical · Motion: assembly/precision/reveal · Photography: cinematic/controlled · Audio: metallic/deep/minimal · Emotion: authority/anticipation/achievement.
