# COMPETITOR RESEARCH VERDICT — MASTER DOCUMENT (Sep 30, 2026)

*Source: 12 research missions, evidence graded A-C, full files in research/competitors/ (ftmo, topstep, tradingview, tradersync, tradezella, myfxbook, tradervue-edgewonk, tradesviz, tradefxbook, prop-firm-graveyard, broker-contests, arena-contests).*

## 1. WHAT TO DO — build priorities (validated)

1. *Journal engine phase 1 stays the flagship.* Every journal competitor's weakness is the same: unreliable imports or paywalls. Free + verified + 15 distilled institutional metrics beats TraderSync's $80/mo, TradesViz's 600-stat overload, Tradervue's 30-trade free cap.
2. *Real-time risk telemetry in the journal dashboard* (FTMO MetriX pattern): live equity curve, drawdown monitoring, parameter warnings.
3. *Payout/prize transparency page* (FTMO/Topstep lesson): every prize pool visible, funded BEFORE competition starts, every payout published.
4. *Anti-cheat engine for competitions* (from broker-contests research): cross-account hedging filter (paired opposite positions near news), latency-arbitrage guard (orders under 120s excluded from scoring), sybil fingerprint filter.
5. *Share cards + trade replay* (TradeZella/TradesViz adopt list) in our design canon.
6. *1-click competition join* (TradingView pattern) once account is verified.

## 2. SCORING — validated by three independent findings

TradingView's "The Leap", broker demo contests, and legacy championships (WCTC/USIC) all rank on raw % returns → reward 100x-leverage gamblers who blow up. Our risk-adjusted Score v1.0 is correct and is THE differentiator. Adopt the research formula as a scoring cross-check: Score = Net PnL% / (1 + Max Drawdown%) × consistency multiplier. Documented, never gamed, never edited by admin — read-only broker data only.

## 3. LESSONS FROM THE GRAVEYARD (rules, enforced forever)

1. MyForexFunds (CFTC, $300M frozen): fake A-book/B-book deception → WE never execute, never custody, never B-book. Read-only forever.
2. The Funded Trader ($1M+ unpaid): paid out prizes from future revenue → prize pools escrowed/funded BEFORE any competition starts.
3. Funded Engineer (faked accounts + payout receipts): every payout and leaderboard claim must be verifiable by the trader — published payout log, no exceptions, no admin edits.
4. FTMO's fee model (75-85% fail and forfeit): we do NOT sell challenges. Revenue = partner + arena side, aligned with members.

## 4. WHAT WE REFUSE (legal + brand shield, absolute)

1. Guru ecosystems, mentorship lounges, signals, "36% monthly returns" marketing (TradeFXBook's world). Never associate, mention, or imitate.
2. Profit promises anywhere. Risk disclaimer on every money surface.
3. Hidden rebills (Topstep complaint) — we have no subscriptions pre-launch; any future pricing shows terms before payment, always.
4. Raw-ROI contests. If it can be won by gambling, it is broken.

## 5. REVIEW-PLATFORM INTELLIGENCE (verified ratings)

TradeZella 4.7 (import bugs = 37% of 1-star), TraderSync 4.3, Topstep 3.5 (15k+ reviews), Myfxbook 2.8 (fake-server exploit), TradeFXBook 2.8-3.1 (~31 reviews, guru-tied), Tradervue free tier crippled, Edgewonk desktop-only. Lesson: journals win on reliability and simplicity, competitions win on trust. We ship both, verified.
