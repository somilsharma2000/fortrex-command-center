## 3. Support Readiness: Predictable Member Ticket Templates

### Ticket 01: Account or Trades Not Syncing
* **Member Issue**: "My MT5 account is connected but recent trades are not showing on the leaderboard or journal."
* **Response Template**:
```text
Hello,

Thank you for reaching out to FORTREX Support.

Trade synchronization relies on periodic read-only API polling between your broker's MT5 server and our journaling engine. If your recent executions are not reflecting:

1. Verify Credentials: Ensure you provided the MT5 'Investor Password' (read-only), not the Master Trading Password.
2. Server Match: Confirm that the selected broker server name matches your live account server exactly.
3. Polling Intervals: During peak volume, sync cycles may take up to 15 minutes to process and index.

If sync remains stalled after 30 minutes, navigate to Settings > Broker Connection and click 'Force Re-sync'. If the issue persists, reply with your MT5 server name and account ID (do NOT share master passwords) for technical inspection.

Regards,
FORTREX Support
```

## 5. Pricing Presentation Options (Counsel-Gated / Draft-Only)


> **Context**: Per REVENUE-MODEL.md, platform-fee structuring is primary to avoid Indian Supreme Court GST Rule 31B (28% GST on total pooled entry fees). Core platform features remain free during Phase 1 pre-launch. Below are 3 honest presentation models for future monetization once counsel signs off.

### Option 1: "100% Free Core Arena" (Platform-Sustained Positioning)
* **Presentation**: "Built for serious traders. Free for participants."
* **Structure**: Core platform access—including MT5 verified leaderboards, automated trade journal, psychology tracking, and open competitions—is 100% free for traders. Platform costs are sustained by institutional partner sponsorships and B2B pipeline integrations.
* **Messaging**:
  > *"FORTREX is free for traders. We believe proving performance should not cost entry fees. Our arena is funded through institutional partnerships with brokers and prop firms seeking pre-verified trading talent."*
* **Pros & Cons**:
  * *Pros*: Maximum user acquisition, zero pricing friction, completely eliminates money-game/gambling classification.
  * *Cons*: Platform relies entirely on closing institutional sponsor agreements before monetization begins.

---

### Option 2: "Freemium Arena Pass" (Modular Depth & Advanced Analytics)
* **Presentation**: "Verification is free. Advanced mastery is modular."
* **Structure**: Free membership includes core MT5 trade verification, live leaderboard placement, and basic journaling. An optional **Arena Pass** (₹299/mo or ₹2,499/yr) unlocks deep psychological analytics, historical profile archives, custom badge cosmetics, and premium tournament brackets.
* **Messaging**:
  > *"Verification on FORTREX is always free. For traders seeking deeper behavioral insights, advanced trade tagging, and historical track record archives, the Arena Pass provides modular analytical power."*
* **Pros & Cons**:
  * *Pros*: Clear SaaS unit economics, 18% GST applies strictly to the SaaS subscription fee (no stake pooling), predictable recurring revenue.
  * *Cons*: Requires proving strong product value for the paid analytics tier without cannibalizing free user retention.

---

### Option 3: "Sponsor-Funded Prize Purses" (Zero Member Entry Fee)
* **Presentation**: "Compete on skill. Sponsored by industry leaders."
* **Structure**: Zero entry fees for any competition. Tournament prize purses and reward pools are funded entirely by third-party sponsors (verified brokers, educational platforms, prop firms).
* **Messaging**:
  > *"You bring the skill; sponsors bring the purse. Competitions on FORTREX carry zero entry fees. Prize pools are funded by institutional partners dedicated to identifying disciplined trading talent."*
* **Pros & Cons**:
  * *Pros*: Highly attractive to traders, zero risk of GST pooling penalties under Rule 31B, clear legal separation between player activity and prize capital.
  * *Cons*: Tournament frequency and purse sizing depend directly on sponsor pipeline velocity and contract sign-offs.
