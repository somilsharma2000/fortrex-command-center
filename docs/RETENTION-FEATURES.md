# FORTREX RETENTION FEATURES — why traders stay for years
> Founder ask, Sep 26: features so traders join for the long run. Grounded in research/universe/02 (psychology), 05 (reward economies), 06 (growth), 07 (tournament design). Rule: every feature builds STATUS, MASTERY, RECORD, or BELONGING. REX Path A law binds all of it.

## TIER 1 — THE RECORDED CAREER (the moat; verified data no one else can copy)
1. **Verified trader record** — every season, every trade, every score, permanently archived. The longer they stay, the more valuable their record becomes. Leaving means abandoning their track history.
2. **Shareable verified profile** — public/copyable verified track record (win rate, drawdown, consistency, seasons played). Usable for prop-firm applications and credibility. FORTREX becomes the trader's professional reference.
3. **Trader levels** — progression by verified seasons and milestones (Initiate → Contender → Proven → Veteran → Master). Visible badge on profile and leaderboard.
4. **Hall of Fame** — season champions permanently recorded. Status that never expires.
5. **Performance graph over time** — your FORTREX score across seasons; see yourself improve. The "improvement on trading" feature the founder asked for.

## TIER 2 — MASTERY ENGINE (the reason to show up weekly)
6. **Post-tournament report card** — after every tournament: what changed vs last time (risk discipline, drawdown, consistency), one concrete strength, one concrete leak. Quiet, honest, no hype.
7. **Weight bracket ladder with promotion** — perform in your class, get promoted to the next bracket next season. Sports model: beginners pool → masters pool. Always something to climb.
8. **Streak insurance** — REX spendable on a streak freeze (vacation protection). Creates a save decision instead of a break decision.
9. **Season championship arc** — Nov 7 launch = Season Zero; regular seasons + an annual Championship. Calendar structure = appointment habit.

## TIER 3 — BELONGING (community without chat chaos)
10. **Educator classrooms** — educators run FORTREX-verified rooms; their students' verified progress is the educator's credibility (ties into PARTNER-PIPELINE).
11. **Trader of the Week feature** — spotlight with interview; quiet institutional voice, record-based, never "profit promises".
12. **Peer benchmarking** — "traders in your bracket averaged X drawdown; you did Y." Comparison creates pull without rankings anxiety.

## TIER 4 — REX DEPTH (Path A safe: benefits, never cash)
13. **REX tiers** — Bronze/Gold/Obsidian status by lifetime REX. Unlocks: early tournament access, bracket priority, profile themes, badge frames.
14. **REX spend on real utility** — extra leaderboard refresh, cosmetic profile upgrades, tournament re-entry (free tournaments only), freeze tokens. Sink keeps the economy alive without a cash exit.

## BUILD ORDER
- Phase 1-2 (Nov-Dec): record + report card + streak insurance + REX tiers (platform data already exists for all four).
- Phase 3 (Dec-Feb): levels + bracket promotion + Hall of Fame + performance graph + benchmarking.
- Phase 4 (2027): educator classrooms + championship arc + shareable export.

## NEVER (retention through addiction tricks is banned)
No loss-leader bonuses, no countdown pressure, no loss aversion mechanics, no cash-value dangles. Retention = the record, the climb, and the room. Scarcity only as fact.
