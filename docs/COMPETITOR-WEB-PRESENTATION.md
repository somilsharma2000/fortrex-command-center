# Competitor Web Presentation — Sep 30, 2026

Direct live reads: tradezella.com, tradefxbook.com, tradervue.com, fxblue.com (myfxbook 403'd; covered from prior research file).

## The 8 presentation patterns every world-level competitor uses

1. **Hero with one-line identity + proof stats.** TradeZella: "AI Trading Partner & #1 Trading Journal" + 100K+ traders, 20.2B trades, 500+ brokers, 4.8★ Trustpilot. TradeFXBook: live stats ticker (Today's PnL, Win Rate).
2. **Product grid.** TradeZella shows 6 named tools (Journaling, Backtesting, Trade Replay, Zella AI, Spaces, Prop Firm Sync) each with icon + one-liner. FORTREX equivalent: Journal, Competitions, Leaderboard, Learn, REX, Community.
3. **Solutions by trader type.** TradeZella segments: unprofitable / developing / profitable / prop / communities. Speaks to each visitor's stage.
4. **Named testimonials + social proof strip.** TradeFXBook uses named Indian trading figures (Umar Punjabi, Trading Katta Dubai). Tradervue's entire homepage is real user quotes. Trustpilot widget common.
5. **Broker/brand logos strip.** "Auto-syncs with 500+ brokers" logos. Ours is MT5-only — legal shield: no partner-broker naming without written approval, so present as "MetaTrader 5" only.
6. **Community + education as first-class sections.** Zella University, Spaces (private rooms, mentor mode, group challenges), TradeFXBook Traders Lounge, FX Blue Discord. We have Learn + planned Discord.
7. **Share cards / public profiles.** TradeFXBook: "generate professional share cards for social media, share read-only access." We ALREADY have public trader profiles (/t/[code]) — a differentiator to present louder.
8. **Transparent pricing page + FAQ + WhatsApp contact.** TradeFXBook: 3 tiers with monthly/yearly toggle, 6-question FAQ, WhatsApp number, response-time promise.

## What FORTREX has vs misses (launch-day checklist)

Have: hero, honest identity, journal, competitions, leaderboard, learn, legal, hall-of-fame, public profiles, REX.
Miss (presentable Nov 7+ without new engineering): stats bar (honest numbers only), feature grid section on landing, trader-type segmentation copy, testimonials (collect post-launch), FAQ on landing, pricing = "free" positioning block, community/Discord section placeholder.

## Legal/canon constraints (binding)

No profit-promise numbers, no fake/testimonials invention, no bait vocabulary (STEALTH-MODE.md), no partner-broker naming pre-approval, quiet institutional design — no ticker theatrics, no giant type. Copy follows CROWN-SYSTEM.md grammar.
