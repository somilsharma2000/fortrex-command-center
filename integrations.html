How connecting works — 30 seconds, no API keys needed

The authorization card handles everything — no API keys, no developer accounts on your side. Three steps:

1 → Tap Connect ↗ on a channel — it opens the FORTREX agent chat.
2 → Send the pre-filled command (e.g. connect instagram) — the authorization card appears immediately.
3 → Approve the card. You get confirmation, this badge flips to Connected, and real-time sync arms: publishing, Insights, and analytics flow straight into the dashboard.

Why the short hop? Authorization must run through the secure agent — a static page can't hold the credentials. The instant the card is approved, everything syncs in real time and your Connect button's status here updates.
