# Platform Format Specs

## Instagram Feed — 1:1 square
- Headline dominant, max 8 words on image, uppercase Space Grotesk style
- Obsidian #050509 background, subtle starfield, controlled glow
- Gold gradient CTA button only when pillar = List Building

## Instagram/X Story — 9:16
- Single idea + gold button at bottom, 70% negative space

## X/Twitter — 16:9 + text
- Text-only manifesto posts in same voice; pinned post = Genesis CTA

## LinkedIn
- Same visuals, slightly more explanatory caption, institutional tone

## YouTube thumbnail — 16:9
- 2–4 word uppercase hook, charcoal + one gold accent
