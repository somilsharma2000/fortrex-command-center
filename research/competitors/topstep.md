# Competitor Analysis: Topstep (topstep.com)

## 1. Features & Pricing

Topstep is widely recognized as the pioneer of the retail futures proprietary trading firm model. It operates a evaluation-to-funded-trader model centered on CME futures instruments (e.g., E-mini S&P 500, Nasdaq, Crude Oil, Gold) with multi-platform connectivity.

### Account Tiers & Evaluation Pricing
Topstep offers two distinct pricing paths for its 1-step **Trading Combine®**:

| Account Size | Profit Target | Max Loss Limit (MLL) | Daily Loss Limit | Standard Path (Monthly) | No Activation Fee Path (Monthly) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **$50,000** | $3,000 | $2,000 | $1,000 | $49/mo (+$149 pass fee) | $95/mo |
| **$100,000** | $6,000 | $3,000 | $2,000 | $99/mo (+$149 pass fee) | $149/mo |
| **$150,000** | $9,000 | $4,500 | $3,000 | $199/mo (+$149 pass fee) | $229/mo |

*Note: Resets cost $49 to $199 depending on account tier and promotional credits, or occur automatically upon monthly rebill.*

### Key Execution & Trading Parameters
* **1-Step Evaluation:** Traders pass by achieving the profit target without hitting the Daily Loss Limit or Maximum Loss Limit.
* **50% Best-Day Consistency Rule:** During the Trading Combine, no single trading day's gain can represent more than 50% of the total profit target. If a single day exceeds 50%, the required total profit target scales dynamically upward.
* **Maximum Loss Limit (Trailing MLL):** Trailing drawdown calculated against equity peak until the account reaches initial balance + $0 (or static MLL threshold depending on product tier).
* **Express Funded Account™ (XFA):** Upon passing the Trading Combine, traders move to a simulated funded account before potential transition to a Live Funded Account.

### Payout Rules & Capital Allocation
* **Payout Split:** 100% of the first $10,000 earned is retained by the trader; 90% profit split thereafter.
* **Payout Eligibility:** Minimum of 5 winning days of $150+ in the Express Funded Account per payout request.
* **Withdrawal Caps:** Payout requests are capped at 50% of the account balance (up to $5,000 per request) until accumulating 30 winning trading days.
* **Processing Speed:** Payouts processed within 1–2 business days via Wise, ACH, or bank transfer.

## 2. Why They Succeeded

Topstep achieved market-leading dominance in the retail futures prop sector through first-mover advantage, aggressive brand building, and continuous UX simplification:

1. **First-Mover Authority & Brand Reputation:** Founded in 2012 by Chicago floor trader Michael Patak, Topstep established trust early in an industry frequently plagued by pop-up operations. Its long operational history creates high barrier-to-entry trust.
2. **Proprietary Technology Stack (TopStepX™):** By introducing TopStepX (a custom web-based execution platform built on Project X technology), Topstep reduced reliance on external brokers/data feeds (like Rithmic/Tradovate) while building native risk management controls directly into the UI.
3. **Transition to 1-Step Evaluation:** Shifting from a complex 2-step process to a single-step Trading Combine radically lowered conversion friction for retail traders, sparking massive subscriber growth.
4. **Community & Media Ecosystem (TopstepTV):** Daily live trading broadcasts, active Discord communities, educational webinars, and transparent founder content transformed evaluation selling into a media and lifestyle brand.
5. **Clear Payout Track Record:** Reliable processing of payouts with no activation fee options reinforced social proof and word-of-mouth viral loops across Reddit, YouTube, and Twitter/X.

## 3. Weaknesses & Complaints

*Sources evaluated: Trustpilot (Score: ~3.5-3.6/5 across 15,000+ reviews), Reddit (`r/Daytrading`, `r/TopStepX`, `r/PropFirmTester`), and trader forums.*

### Evaluated Complaints & Honesty Grading

| Complaint / Friction Point | Detailed Evidence & User Context | Source Verification | Honesty Grade |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **50% Consistency Rule Friction** | Traders who hit massive winning days during evaluations find their profit target increased rather than passing, creating confusion and extended subscription rebills. | Official Rules & Reddit (`r/Daytrading`) | **Grade A** (Fully Verified) |
| **Trailing Max Loss Limit Traps** | Trailing drawdown locked on unrealized intraday peak profit penalizes traders who let winning trades pull back, causing unexpected auto-liquidations. | User manuals & Trustpilot reviews | **Grade A** (Fully Verified) |
| **Subscription Rebill Friction** | Recurring monthly billing continues automatically if account fails or stalls unless manually canceled or reset, leading to billing complaints. | Trustpilot reviews & Help Center | **Grade A** (Fully Verified) |
| **Platform Outages & Data Delays** | Intermittent connection drops or latency during high-volatility news events (FOMC, CPI) on third-party feeds (Rithmic/Tradovate) leading to slippage. | Reddit threads (`r/SierraChart`, `r/TopstepCommunity`) | **Grade B** (Well-documented qualitative evidence) |
| **Support Delays During Volatility** | Support ticket response times slow down significantly during market turbulence or system migration events. | Trustpilot negative reviews | **Grade B** (Moderate operational variance) |
| **Allegations of Intentional Order Manipulation / "Rigged" Liquidation** | Occasional user claims that liquidations occur deliberately to harvest reset/subscription fees. | Unsubstantiated forum rants | **Grade C** (Unverified / Retail trader bias) |

*Honesty Grade Legend:*
* **Grade A:** Factually verified through published official policy, platform mechanics, or standard contractual terms.
* **Grade B:** Supported by consistent, multi-source trader reports; plausible operational limitation.
* **Grade C:** Unverifiable individual anecdote, emotional post-loss reaction, or unsupported accusation.
