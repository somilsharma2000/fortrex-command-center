# Competitor Analysis: Tradervue & Edgewonk

## Tradervue (tradervue.com)

### Features & Pricing
- **Pricing Tiers:**
  - **Free Tier (Basic):** Limited to 30 trade imports per month, basic journaling, and basic PnL stats.
  - **Silver Plan ($29.95/month):** Unlimited trade imports, auto-import from supported brokers, detailed execution statistics, trade tagging, and interactive price charts with execution overlays.
  - **Gold Plan ($49.95/month):** Includes all Silver features plus advanced Exit Analysis, Risk Analysis (R-multiple metrics, drawdowns), Liquidity Metrics, and Tag Analytics.
  - **Enterprise API:** Offers dedicated institutional API connectivity for prop trading firms, funds, and institutional desks requiring automated trade ingestion, multi-user management, and custom reporting pipelines.
- **Core Functionality:**
  - Automated trade import via broker APIs and file uploads.
  - Visual trade execution plotting on candlestick charts.
  - Public trade sharing capabilities and mentor/coaching views.
  - Performance filtering by date, ticker, tag, or market condition.

### Strengths
- **Institutional & Pioneer Heritage:** Longstanding presence in professional trade journaling with established credibility among prop firms and retail traders.
- **Enterprise API Connectivity:** Robust backend for institutional clients and prop desks seeking programmatically synced trade data.
- **Execution Analytics:** Deep focus on execution analysis, entry/exit timing, slip analysis, and R-multiple risk metrics.
- **Social Sharing:** Simple URL sharing for trade logs, enabling community feedback and mentoring workflows.

### Weaknesses & Complaints
- **Billing & Cancellation Friction (Grade: C- | Source: Trustpilot):** High volume of negative reviews (~2.6/5 overall rating on Trustpilot, with ~88% of negative reviews citing billing disputes, hidden auto-renewals, difficult cancellation processes, and unresponsive customer service).
- **Outdated UI/UX (Grade: C | Source: User Reviews & Software Comparison Sites):** Visual presentation and user interface remain largely legacy/tabular, lacking the modern responsive design expected in contemporary SaaS platforms like TradeZella or TraderSync.
- **Restrictive Free Tier (Grade: C+ | Source: Tradervue Site & Community Forums):** The 30-trade monthly limit renders the free plan impractical for active or intraday traders, functioning primarily as a restrictive trial that forces paid conversion.
- **Absence of Behavioral Psychology Tools (Grade: C | Source: Feature Comparison Analysis):** Lacks structured emotional tracking, tilt meters, or psychological habit tagging compared to Edgewonk.

### What FORTREX Adopts
- **Verified Automated Broker Import:** Adopt automated MT5 broker synchronization for seamless execution logging without manual CSV friction.
- **R-Multiple & Entry/Exit Visualization:** Adopt execution plotting and risk analytics to show precise trade mechanics and risk management performance.
- **Enterprise-Grade Infrastructure:** Build scalable data structures and API hooks compatible with prop firm integrations and team leaderboards.
- **FORTREX Improvement:** Deliver all flagship journaling and analytics features **100% free for FORTREX members**, eliminating Tradervue's $30–$50/month paywalls, 30-trade monthly caps, and predatory billing friction.

## Edgewonk (edgewonk.com)

### Features & Pricing
- **Pricing Model:**
  - **Flat Annual Subscription:** $169/year ($14.08/month equivalent, billed annually).
  - No free tier, no monthly payment option, and no free trial.
- **Platform Architecture & Core Functionality:**
  - **Edgewonk 3 (Web/Cloud Platform):** Shifted from legacy standalone desktop software (Edgewonk 2) to a cloud-hosted web application supporting multi-asset journaling (forex, stocks, futures, crypto, indices).
  - **Behavioral Psychology Layer:** Features the proprietary "Tilt Meter", mistake tracking, emotional state logs, and discipline scores.
  - **Trade Simulator & Optimization:** Monte Carlo simulation engine, equity curve projection, and "what-if" trade management testing (evaluating performance with adjusted stop-losses or targets).
  - **Custom Metrics & Proprietary Scoring:** Custom tracking variables and proprietary formulas (e.g., Edgewonk SQN / System Quality Number).

### Strengths
- **Behavioral & Psychological Focus:** Market leader in quantifying trader psychology, emotional bias, discipline adherence, and mistake costing.
- **Advanced Trade Simulation:** Powerful scenario modeling ("what-if" analysis) allowing traders to test strategic adjustments on past data.
- **Predictable Flat Pricing:** Single subscription tier ($169/yr) covering all features without tiered feature gating or trade volume limits.
- **Customization Depth:** Highly flexible tagging, custom variables, and multi-asset versatility.

### Weaknesses & Complaints
- **Forced Web Migration & Desktop Legacy Discontent (Grade: C+ | Source: Trustpilot & Reddit):** Widespread dissatisfaction among long-time Edgewonk 2 desktop users regarding the mandatory shift to Edgewonk 3's cloud subscription model, loss of offline desktop capabilities, and initial web app stability/sync issues.
- **Lack of Native Real-Time Broker Auto-Sync (Grade: B- | Source: User Reviews & Platform Comparison Sites):** Heavy reliance on manual file/CSV uploads or third-party file converters rather than frictionless, real-time automated broker API synchronization.
- **High Barrier to Entry / Paywall (Grade: C+ | Source: Edgewonk Site & User Feedback):** Requiring an upfront $169 annual commitment with zero free trial or monthly subscription option deters beginner and budget-conscious traders.

### What FORTREX Adopts
- **Behavioral Psychology Layer:** Adopt emotional state logging, mistake tags, discipline scoring, and psychological tilt alerts.
- **Optimization & Scenario Modeling:** Adopt "what-if" trade management analysis to help members evaluate execution discipline against theoretical optimal strategies.
- **Multi-Asset Versatility:** Support comprehensive tracking across forex, indices, commodities, and futures.
- **FORTREX Improvement:** Combine Edgewonk's psychological depth with **native MT5 real-time auto-sync** (removing manual CSV headaches) and offer the entire suite **free of charge** within the FORTREX ecosystem, backed by gamified REX rewards, competition leaderboards, and an active Discord community.
