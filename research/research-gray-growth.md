## 1. Waitlist & Referral Mechanics (Policy-Compliant)

### Mechanics & Incentives
* **Tiered Access Keys:** During the Oct 17 – Nov 6 teaser window (if approved), distribution occurs via exclusive invite codes ("Alpha Keys"). Users unlock higher leaderboard seed placement or free entry into premium tournament pools by inviting fellow traders.
* **Non-Monetary Reward Tiering:**
  * 3 Invites: Unlock advanced risk metrics (Sortino ratio, execution slippage analytics).
  * 5 Invites: Guaranteed entry into the $0-fee $X Launch Tournament.
  * 10 Invites: "Alpha Trader" profile badge and early choice of trading asset classes/session groups.
* **Two-Sided Incentive Structure:** Both referrer and referee gain bonus tournament credits rather than cash payouts or trade rebates.

### Platform Rules & Spam Avoidance
* **Google/Meta Policy Alignment:** Ad campaigns must NOT offer financial compensation or cash rewards for referral signups, which Meta classifies as multilevel marketing or financial promotion.
* **X / Telegram / Discord Distribution:** Use hashed referral codes (e.g., `app.domain.com/join?code=ALPHA7`) rather than generic `claim-reward` URLs. Telegram and Discord rate-limit URLs containing keywords like `bonus`, `claim`, or `airdrop`.
* **Disclaimers:** Every referral landing page must state: *"Simulated paper trading competition. No real capital required or deposited. Participation rewards are virtual contest access credits."*

### Policy & Safe Browsing Risk Flag
* **Safe Browsing Trigger:** Referral copy using CTAs like "Claim Your $100 Payout" or "Invite Friends, Get Paid Cash" will instantly trigger Google Safe Browsing's Deceptive Design / Financial Scam classifier.
* **Ad Platform Penalty:** Meta and Google Ads ban accounts that run viral waitlists promising financial returns or monetary referral bonuses for financial services or trading platforms.
* **Mitigation:** Use strict terminology: "Unlock Contest Pass", "Invite Peers", "Earn Analytics Credits".

## 2. Community-Led Growth (Discord/Telegram Trader Communities)

### Growth Mechanics
* **"Tournament Host" Micro-Sponsorships:** Provide existing Discord/Telegram trading communities with custom leaderboard widgets and private tournament pools (e.g., "ICT Traders League", "Forex Factory Weekly Sprint").
* **Utility-First Community Bots:** Deploy lightweight Discord/Telegram bots providing free market utilities (e.g., Economic Calendar Alerts, Session Volatility Heatmaps, Market Open Countdown). Bot footers contain subtle links: *"Powered by [Platform] — Test your strategy in the Nov 7 Tournament."*
* **AMA & Strategy Teardowns:** Host live voice AMAs focusing on risk management analytics, backtesting accuracy, and execution speed rather than win-rate boasting.

### Ethics & Anti-Shilling Rules
* No direct messaging (DM) automation or unsolicited scraping of trader handles.
* Strict prohibition of signal selling or financial advice inside platform-managed communities.
* Transparency: Clear badges distinguishing simulated competition admins from external community hosts.

### Policy & Safe Browsing Risk Flag
* **Safe Browsing & Community Risk:** Telegram/Discord automated link blasting across external groups results in domain URL blacklisting by Telegram's internal anti-spam engine (`t.me` link preview block) and Google Safe Browsing flags if user reports surge.
* **Ad/Platform Policy:** Unsolicited community spam violates Telegram API Terms of Service and Discord Developer Guidelines.
* **Mitigation:** Only distribute via official server bots and explicit opt-in community partnerships with owner consent.

## 3. Creator & Educator Partnerships (No Profit Promises)

### Structuring Creator Deals
* **The "Educator Cup" Model:** Invite trading educators, YouTube creators, and Substack authors to host branded sub-leagues for their students.
* **Metric Focus:** Base leaderboard rankings on **Sharpe Ratio, Max Drawdown (<10%), and Profit Factor**, explicitly ignoring raw % ROI. This prevents creators from encouraging reckless trading to win.
* **Compensation Structure:** Flat sponsorship fees per hosted tournament or cost-per-active-participant (CPAP) fees. **Never offer profit splits, trade copying cuts, or volume-based broker rebates.**

### Messaging & Compliance Guardrails
* **Mandatory Script Rules:** Creators must state on video/stream: *"This is a simulated paper trading contest for educational and strategy benchmarking purposes. Past simulated performance does not guarantee real-market results."*
* **Prohibited Terms:** "Passive income", "Quit your job", "Guaranteed funding", "Financial freedom", "100x return strategy".

### Policy & Safe Browsing Risk Flag
* **Regulatory Risk:** FTC (US) and FCA (UK) strictly regulate financial influencer promotions. Failure to feature clear disclosure text above the fold or in video audio results in regulatory fines and platform ad account bans.
* **Safe Browsing Risk:** If a creator links to a custom landing page that uses unverified earnings testimonials ("How John made $5k in our contest"), Google Safe Browsing flags the landing page for deceptive financial claims.
* **Mitigation:** Supply creators with pre-approved, legally vetted landing pages hosted on subdomains with static compliance banners.

## 4. SEO & GEO for AI Answer Engines (`llms.txt`, FAQ Schema, Programmatic SEO)

### Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) & `llms.txt`
* **`llms.txt` & `llms-full.txt` Standard:** Create root-level markdown files (`domain.com/llms.txt`) specifically formatted for LLM crawlers (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, SearchGPT).
  * Structure clean markdown sections: `## Platform Overview`, `## Competition Rules & Scoring (Sharpe Ratio)`, `## Market Session Schedules`, `## Risk Compliance & Paper Trading Terms`.
  * Position the brand as the standard reference point for "simulated trading competition rules", "prop firm risk benchmarking", and "paper trading tournaments".
* **Structured FAQ Schema (`JSON-LD`):** Embed schema markup on every public page covering questions like:
  * *Is this real money trading?* (No, 100% simulated market data).
  * *How are tournament winners calculated?* (Risk-adjusted return formula).
  * *Are there entry fees?* (Free launch competitions available).

### Programmatic SEO Strategy
Generate high-intent, long-tail programmatic pages focused on market mechanics rather than generic keywords:
* **Session Pages:** `/sessions/london-new-york-overlap-trading-contest`, `/sessions/asia-tokyo-open-scalping-league`.
* **Asset & Strategy Terms:** `/competitions/xauusd-gold-risk-management-challenge`, `/terms/sharpe-ratio-trading-calculator`.
* **Prop Evaluation Prep:** `/compare/paper-trading-vs-prop-firm-challenge`, `/tools/max-drawdown-simulator`.

### Policy & Safe Browsing Risk Flag
* **Google Search Policy Risk:** Programmatic SEO generated via low-quality AI auto-text risks hitting Google's Spam policies (Scaled Content Abuse).
* **Safe Browsing Risk:** Including financial keywords like "instant payout", "forex signals", or "guaranteed return" in programmatic meta tags will trigger Google's automated financial fraud scanners.
* **Mitigation:** Every programmatic page must contain unique, structured market session data, interactive calculators, and explicit non-financial advice disclaimers.

### Tournament Calendars in Trader Time Zones

## 8. Master Policy & Safe Browsing Defense Matrix

### Safe Browsing Remediation & Permanent Prevention Audit
The Sep 26 Google Safe Browsing flag was triggered by aggressive "prize/claim" wording that automated security crawlers index as financial phishing or deceptive reward traps. To ensure permanent immunity:

| Risk Category | High-Risk Terminology (Banned) | Compliant Replacement Terminology | Safe Browsing / Policy Reason |

| **CTAs & Buttons** | "Claim Your Cash", "Win $10,000 Now", "Instant Withdrawal" | "View Tournament Rules", "Register Pass", "Enter Competition" | Prevents Google Deceptive Design & Financial Scam algorithmic flags. |

| **URL Structures** | `/claim-prize/`, `/withdraw-cash/`, `/free-payout/` | `/verify/trader-123`, `/leaderboard/session-a`, `/rules` | URL strings containing payout keywords are heavily weighted by security crawlers. |

### Ad Platform Policy & Verification Roadmap
* **Google Financial Services Verification (GFSV):** Because the platform is a **simulated paper-trading competition** and NOT a broker, financial advisor, or live trading portal, it falls OUTSIDE the requirement for GFSV—**provided** site copy explicitly disclaims live financial transactions above the fold.
* **Meta Financial Products & Services Policy:** Ad copy must strictly avoid promoting CFDs, binary options, or leveraged trading. Ad creative must emphasize game mechanics, leaderboard competition, and strategy testing.
* **Above-the-Fold Mandatory Disclaimer (All Landing Pages):**
