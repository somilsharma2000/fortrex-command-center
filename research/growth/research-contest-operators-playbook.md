## SECTION 2: Playbook Extraction for Scaling to 100k+ Users

### Top Scale Operator Playbook (0 to 100k+ Users)
1. **Frictionless Zero-Capital Onboarding:** Top operators allow traders to join free contests in under 30 seconds using paper/simulated accounts or automated API connections before requiring live broker registration `[SOURCED: research-gray-growth.md]`.
2. **Viral Shareable Proof-of-Skill Artifacts:** Automated generation of high-resolution social media cards (OG Images) showing verified win rate, Sharpe ratio, and drawdown, allowing traders to flex achievements without triggering spam flags `[SOURCED: research-gray-growth.md]`.
3. **Decentralized Distribution via Community Leads (Educator League):** Partnering with trading academies and Discord/Telegram group owners who run custom co-branded leagues for their existing student bases `[SOURCED: research-gray-growth.md]`.
4. **Automated API-Based Account Mapping:** Eliminating manual submission by querying broker postback APIs / MT4/MT5 server manager feeds to instantly verify whether a live trading account is active under the partner tree `[SOURCED: research-xm-partner.md]`.

### Critical Mistakes to Avoid
* **Promoting "Guaranteed Returns" or "Cash Bonuses":** Triggers immediate Google Safe Browsing deceptive site flags, Meta ad bans, and regulatory enforcement `[SOURCED: research-gray-growth.md]`.
* **Rewarding Raw PnL Percentage Without Risk Limits:** Encourages 100x leverage gambling where a trader wins 10,000% on one lucky trade, destroying platform credibility for serious traders `[ASSUMPTION]`.
* **Relying Solely on Manual Verification:** Manual email/screenshot checking creates operational bottlenecks that fail beyond 500 active users `[ASSUMPTION]`.
* **Over-Dependence on a Single Broker:** Sudden broker affiliate policy changes or payout delays can freeze platform cash flow `[SOURCED: research-gray-revenue.md]`.
