## Section 5: North-Star Metric Choice

### 1. Defining the Primary North Star Metric
For FORTREX, raw registrations or total website visits are vanity metrics. In a trading competition platform, liquidity and active participation drive retention and monetization.

> **Primary North Star Metric:** **Verified Active Competing Traders (VACT)**  
> *Definition:* Unique traders who complete at least one verified tournament trading round (minimum 3 completed trades) per 30-day rolling window.

```
                  NORTH STAR TELEMETRY STRUCTURE
  
                 Verified Active Competing Traders (VACT)
                                 │
         ┌───────────────────────┴───────────────────────┐
         ▼                                               ▼
Weekly Competition Volume (WCV)             Partner Referral Efficiency (PRE)
[Tournament Liquidity & Matches]            [Active Signups per Partner Code]
```

### 2. Why Velocity / Momentum Metrics Fit Tournament Products
Tournament products depend on **liquidity density**. If a trader enters a contest and waits hours for match-making or leaderboard updates, interest drops. Velocity metrics measure operational momentum:
- **Velocity of Tournament Entry (VTE)**: Time elapsed from landing page signup to entering the first tournament (Target: < 10 minutes).
- **Match Liquidity Rate (MLR)**: Percentage of daily tournament pools that fill to minimum capacity within 15 minutes of opening (Target: > 90%). [Grade D]

### 3. Supporting Telemetry Metrics to Instrument Day One

| Metric Name | Target Benchmark | Strategic Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
