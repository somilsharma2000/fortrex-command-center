2. Reality check: what the research changed

Read this section carefully. It changes the plan.

1. UK and EU cannot join the LIVE cash contests

UK (FCA) and EU (ESMA) rules ban cash prizes and incentives tied to live retail CFD trading. UK and EU traders can join DEMO contests only. So "London / UK" cannot be a live-prize market.

2. US and India are blocked from the live track

US: CFTC and state contest laws. India: the 2025 online gaming law plus FEMA/RBI (XM is on the RBI alert list). You live in India, so the company that signs broker deals should sit offshore (UAE or Singapore), not in India.

3. We cannot show offices, people or locations that do not exist

A "London office" or fake team photos is a false statement. It risks another Google flag and legal trouble. We can honestly show: worldwide service, real market sessions (Sydney, Tokyo, London, New York), and a real UAE entity once it exists.

4. We cannot promise earnings

"Traders can earn here" is the fastest way to get flagged or sued. We promise learning, community, verified proof of skill, and free contests. Prizes exist, but nobody is told they will earn.

5. The research suggested word-swaps to dodge flags. I rejected that.

One report proposed calling tournaments "Benchmark Evaluations" and prizes "Honoraria". That disguises the product and looks more suspicious to regulators. We keep mystery in what we reveal and when, but never rename real things to hide them.

What works well

  • The broker pays prizes directly. FORTREX never touches prize money (removes most tax and gaming-law exposure for us).
  • Best first markets: Gulf states, Latin America, Southeast Asia.
  • Demo contests can run almost everywhere, including UK and EU.

3. How live MT5 works (nothing for you to code)

Plain steps. Two are yours, the rest is built.

  1. MetaApi (metaapi.cloud): a service that reads MT5 accounts. You make one account and give me its API token. you, 10 min
  2. Trader connects: types MT5 login, server, and the investor password. That password is read-only: we can see trades and balance, never place or withdraw. built
  3. XM partner account: you apply as an XM partner and get a partner link and code. I paste the link in the admin settings. you
  4. Verification: the trader submits their MT5 login as opened under our code; admin approves it against the XM partner portal (which shows client IDs and account numbers). built
  5. Scoring: automatic, every few minutes, USD-normalized, reproducible. built

Honest note: I have never connected to a real MetaApi account, only the mock. First real connection is a test I will do the day you give the token.

10. The free AI trading journal

We studied 10 tools: TraderSync, Edgewonk, TradeZella, TradesViz, Kinfo, Tradervue, FXReplay, Myfxbook, Trademetria, Chartlog.

What traders hate about them

  • Sync breaks or needs a desktop program running
  • Generic AI ("manage your risk")
  • Price hikes, hard cancellations
  • Cluttered or outdated screens
  • Real AI locked behind the top price tier
  • Public boards that leak strategies

What we do instead

  • Automatic read-only import, nothing to install
  • AI that names specific patterns in YOUR trades (revenge trading, overtrading after losses, size creep, bad sessions)
  • Free core, extras earned with REX
  • Clean phone-first screens
  • Private by default, share on purpose

Reports

  • Per-trade review
  • Session and pair breakdown
  • Risk of ruin
  • Weekly report to Discord or email
  • Behavior score over time

The AI writes feedback about YOUR trades and risk habits. It never says buy or sell, and every report carries "educational, not advice".
