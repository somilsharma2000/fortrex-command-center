## 1. The math (why posts alone cannot fill 10,000)

- A solo account with no audience posting launch content: 50-200 signups, best case.
- 10,000 seats needs either a large existing audience (we don't have one, by design) or
  **borrowed audiences**.
- One mid-size trading educator (5k-50k followers) with real trust can deliver 100-500
  signups from one honest mention.
- **Target: 30 partners × ~300 avg = 9,000 + direct private invites ≈ 10,000.** That's the
  whole model. Every partner's audience overlaps ~30% — which is why we recruit 30, not 10.

## 2. Who to recruit (the partner profile — strict criteria)

**YES (the top 20% that bring 80%):**
- Trading educators/YouTubers/Telegram channel owners with **5k-50k** followers (big
  enough to matter, small enough to say yes and to reply to a DM)
- Post **verifiable results** (journal screenshots, live sessions) — they already hate
  the fake-screenshot culture, which is FORTREX's exact enemy
- Audiences that are 80%+ Indian (launch focus; RBI compliance is easier when the
  market is domestic)
- Trust-driven, low-hype tone themselves

**NO:**
- Anyone selling signals/courses with "guaranteed profit" language (brand poison + legal
  adjacency)
- Mega-influencers (no reply, no fit, expensive)
- Non-trading audiences

**Where the founder finds them (15-minute daily routine, Oct 1-15):**
- YouTube search: "forex trading India", "price action Hindi", "trading journal" → note
  channels 5k-50k subs that post verified content
- Telegram/Discord trading groups he's already in → owners/moderators
- His own DMs: the first 25 private invitees — ask each "who in your circle teaches or
  runs a room?"

## 3. The offer (why a partner says yes)

They get the rare thing: **founding partner status** —
1. Their own Genesis seat + 1.25x permanent multiplier (already valuable to them personally)
2. A **partner code** — every signup through it is permanently attributed; at launch
   they can show their community "I brought the 4th largest founding cohort"
3. Early access at T-3 days: they see the platform before anyone, so their Nov 7 mention
   is informed and genuine
4. Post-launch: first tournament co-host slots (their community vs. the arena — a ready
   content format for their channel)

What we do NOT offer: money, revenue share (yet — revisit post-launch when the model is
legal-cleared), or exclusivity. Honesty is the pitch: "no hype brand launching; you'll see
it before you endorse it."

## 4. The outreach scripts (send as-is, personalized with the name)

**The follow-up (what it is, one screen):**
> "FORTREX — skill-based trading tournaments where your capital stays at your own broker.

**If they ask "what's in it for me":** the §3 list, plainly.

**Rules:** one follow-up after 4 days, then stop. Never pressure. Log every contact in the
partner tracker (below). Anyone who leaks publicly before Nov 7 loses founding partner
status — say this gently, once.
