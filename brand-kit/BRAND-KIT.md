# FORTREX FX Brand Kit — Master Style & Voice Guide

Use this skill whenever creating ANY content for FORTREX FX: posts, stories, captions, hashtags, thumbnails, banners, emails, landing copy. Every asset must match the official site (https://somilsharma2000.github.io/fortrexfxmanusgold/).

## Brand Core

- **Name:** FORTREX FX
- **Tagline:** "WHERE TRADERS RISE."
- **Phase:** Genesis Early Access (pre-launch). Positioning: exclusive, mysterious, premium. "For traders who recognize the feeling of being early."
- **Primary CTA:** "JOIN THE GENESIS LIST" (always uppercase, always with ↗ arrow when space allows)
- **Launch marker:** "— LAUNCH / 001 —" (used as an eyebrow/label motif)

## Visual Identity (must match site exactly)

- **Background:** Deep charcoal black `#0a0a0a` with a subtle radial glow toward center and a faint starfield/static texture. Never pure black, never flat gray.
- **Headlines:** Off-white `#f4f0e6`, bold, SANS-SERIF, ALL UPPERCASE, generous letter width. Multi-line stacking preferred over one long line.
- **Accents / CTAs:** Gold-to-tan gradient `#d4b483` (gold fading to slightly darker tan), rounded-rectangle buttons, black uppercase text on the gold fill.
- **Labels / eyebrows:** Small, uppercase, letter-spaced (tracked-out), tan/gold color.
- **Body text:** Muted grey, clean medium-weight sans-serif, centered.
- **Aesthetic rules:** Extreme minimalism. High contrast. Lots of negative space. One idea per asset. Luxury, not clutter. No stock-photo collages, no rainbow colors, no lowercase casual fonts.

## Voice & Tone

- Confident, quiet, exclusive. Speaks like a private club, not a hustle-culture page.
- Short declarative lines. Think manifesto, not brochure.
- NEVER: hype-bro language ("🚀 100x gains!!"), emojis as decoration, exclamation spam, financial promises, "get rich" framing.
- Motifs that work: being early, rising, ascent, the few who know, doors opening, genesis, first names.
- Traders rise — the brand elevates discipline and patience, not greed.

## Content Pillars (Genesis pre-launch phase)

1. **The Tease** — cryptic countdown/launch marker posts ("LAUNCH / 001")
2. **Trader Mindset** — minimal quote cards on discipline, patience, timing
3. **Exclusivity** — "the first names will know before everyone else"
4. **Brand Craft** — logo/crest spotlight, gold-on-black texture close-ups
5. **List Building** — Genesis List CTA posts with a clear reason to join now

## Platform Formats

- **IG Feed:** 1:1 square, headline-dominant, max 8 words of copy on the image
- **IG/X Stories:** 9:16, single idea + swipe-up/link CTA at bottom in gold button
- **X/Twitter:** 16:9 banner + text-only posts using the same manifesto voice; pinned post = Genesis CTA
- **LinkedIn:** slightly more explanatory copy, same visual language
- **YouTube:** thumbnail = giant 2-4 word uppercase hook on charcoal + gold accent

## Caption Formula

1. Hook line (manifesto voice, 1 line)
2. 1-2 lines of tension/desire (the feeling of being early)
3. CTA: "Join the Genesis List." + link
4. Hashtag block: keep tight and tiered (see below)

## Hashtag Sets

**Core (every post):** #FortrexFX #WhereTradersRise #GenesisEarlyAccess
**Trading reach:** #TradingCommunity #ForexTrader #TraderLifestyle #TradingMindset #ForexTrading #DayTrading
**Luxury/premium:** #LuxuryLifestyle #MinimalDesign #DarkAesthetic
**Rule:** max 10-12 hashtags, mix 2 core + 5-6 reach + 2-3 aesthetic. Never generic spam tags.

## Quality Gate (before publishing anything)

- [ ] Charcoal + off-white + gold gradient only?
- [ ] Uppercase bold headline present?
- [ ] Minimal, high negative space?
- [ ] Voice = quiet confidence, zero hype-bro?
- [ ] CTA present where appropriate?
- [ ] Alt text written (use visual-media skill)?
