# FORTREX — Revenue Model (One-Pager, Honest Numbers)

## The core math discovery (why this matters more than anything else)

If FORTREX pools entry fees for prize money, the Supreme Court's Gameskraft ruling +
Rule 31B mean **28% GST is due on the FULL entry fee, not our cut**:

| Model | Player pays | GST dies to govt | Prize pool available |
|-------|-------------|------------------|----------------------|
| Entry-fee pool (₹1,000 x 1,000 players) | ₹10,00,000 | **₹2,80,000 (28%)** | ₹7,20,000 minus our rake |
| Platform-fee / sponsor model | ₹10,00,000 (subs + sponsorships) | ₹1,80,000 (18% SaaS on our fee only) | Sponsor funds prizes separately |

Entry-fee pooling is economically crushed before we start. The structuring question is
**#1 on the counsel brief** (REX-ECONOMY.md §4): can a sponsor/platform-fee model with
separately-funded prizes keep us out of "online money game" classification entirely?

## Phase 1 — Foundation (Nov 7 2026 → Jan 2027): ₹0 revenue, on purpose

Free entry. 10,000 Genesis seats. All stack tiers = ₹0 (verified hard-stop, no surprise
bills). Burn = domain (~₹1,000/yr) + trademark (~₹18,000 once) + legal consult
(~₹15-25k). **~₹40k total to launch.** This phase buys the asset: verified traders +
the trusted leaderboard. FTMO needed ~$213M/yr turnover to exist; we need a leaderboard
people believe.

## Phase 2 — Arena Pass (Q1 2027, counsel-gated): the subscription spine

- **Arena Pass**: ₹299/mo (₹2,499/yr) — premium tournaments, deep stats, historical
  profile, badge/identity features. 18% GST on the fee, no stake pooling.
- Honest sensitivity at 10,000 Genesis members (research benchmarks, Grade B/D):
  3% conversion = 300 subs = ₹90k MRR gross. 8% = ₹2.4L MRR. Not wealth — proof of
  willingness to pay, which is what the seed round needs.
- Free tier never gutted: verified leaderboard stays free. Paying adds depth, not truth.
