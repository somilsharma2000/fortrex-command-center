# FORTREX — Support, Refund & Disputes (Launch Pack)
> Drafted Sep 27, 2026. Founder creates the mailbox when ready (any free provider works
> during stealth; moves to support@fortrex.io at domain). No infrastructure needed.

## Channels

1. **Support email** (primary, launch): support@fortrex.io. Founder checks twice daily
   first two weeks; SLA target: respond within 24h, even if only "received, investigating."
2. **Citadel room** (community): peer answers, moderator = founder/agent; escalate to email.
3. **In-app**: no chatbot at launch. Honesty beats automation. A plain "email us" beats
   a bad bot, especially for a trust-brand.

## Ticket types + first-response templates

| Type | First response must contain |
|------|------------------------------|
| Login/account | acknowledge + timeline |
| Broker connection | "read-only, we never store your password" reassurance + reset steps |
| Verification stuck | sync status explanation + retry steps |
| Scoring dispute | route to appeal process (48h window, written reasons) |
| Fraud report | never discuss other users; confirm received; review is private |

Template law: short, human, no hype, no promises of outcomes, risk disclaimer on any
money-related reply. Two written templates (connection issue + dispute) ready to paste.

## Refund policy (DRAFT — applies to any paid feature; nothing is paid at launch)

1. Arena Pass subscriptions: full refund within 7 days, no questions asked, if fewer
   than 3 tournaments entered. Cancellation: self-serve, one click, no retention maze
   (dark-pattern law).
2. Tournament ticket refunds (if ever permitted): entry refunded if tournament cancels
   before going live. No refunds after `live` (the competition ran).
3. Never: automatic renewals without notice; cancellation friction; "contact support
   to cancel" (self-serve always works).

All refunds are counsel-reviewed with the payments legal gate; amounts refund to source.

## Disputes (from TOURNAMENT-SPEC)

48h appeal window · written evidence from both sides (our broker records are the truth
source) · human review, no AI verdicts · decision with reasons · final is final ·
corrections versioned and announced, never silent.

## Escalation path

Agent monitors + heartbeat (automated) → daily digests → anything the automated
systems can't answer = founder. Legal complaints or regulator contact: founder
immediately, no public reply, counsel looped in same day.
