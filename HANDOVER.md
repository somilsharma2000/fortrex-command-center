## 2. The vision (say it like this)

**FORTREX is a sovereign arena where proven trading skill is the only currency that matters.**

In an industry built on hype, fake screenshots, and prop firms that hold your money and
invent reasons to deny your payout, FORTREX inverts the model: the trader's capital stays
at their own broker, always. FORTREX never touches funds. It runs the arena — the
tournaments, the honest leaderboards, the risk-adjusted scoring, the rewards — and the
truth of the results is verified, not self-reported. A trader with a $500 account competes
on fair terms with $50,000 through weight brackets, because the arena measures *skill*,
not account size.

**The founding mechanic:** 10,000 Genesis seats, allocated before anyone else can enter.
Founding members carry a permanent 1.25x REX multiplier and a status that can never be
purchased later. Scarcity that is real, not manufactured.

**The business model:** free entry at launch → REX (a reputation-and-rewards currency with
no cash value — a deliberate legal shield) → paid tournaments later, after the legal
consult. Revenue comes from arena fees, never from holding or trading client money.

**The operating philosophy — this is the part the founder cannot say in words but feels
completely:** the business must be a machine, not a job. Content flows from written packs.
Signups flow into a database without a human touching them. Referrals credit themselves.
Reports arrive every Monday morning without anyone asking. The site heals, scales, and
deploys itself on managed infrastructure with zero servers. The design system is encoded
once, in tokens, so everything produced is automatically on-brand. The goal is that a
normal, non-technical person could operate and profit from this business by making
decisions — while the machine does the work. Every choice you make must move FORTREX
further from "needs the founder" and closer to "runs itself."

**The psychology (already researched and encoded):** institutional calm beats hype.
Silence is a strategy. Stealth until launch means the entire market's curiosity releases
in one loud moment on November 7, and the first impression is a finished arena, not a
landing page. Private founding-seat invites convert at 60-80% because a personal seat is
a gift; a public waitlist form is homework.
