## 2. The legal guardrail (read once, never forget)

From the legal research (`research/04-legal-and-business-setup.md`): promoting **offshore
forex brokers to Indian residents is the AVOID zone** (FEMA/RBI). So:

- **India-facing partners** must teach **NSE/BSE/MCX markets** (equities, options, indices, commodities). These are the markets your Indian audience can legally trade.
- **Forex/CFD educators** are fine ONLY for the global (non-India) track — and only after launch, when geography handling exists. Park them in a separate list; do not recruit them for the November launch wave.
- This is not paranoia — it is the difference between a business and an Enforcement Directorate notice.

## 4. The finding playbook (fill the list to 50)

**YouTube** (best signal-to-noise):
- Search: `"price action" trading India Hindi` · `NIFTY options trading strategy` · `trading journal India` · `stock market basics Hindi chart`
- Filter: This month → look for channels between 5k–50k subs that post **real chart breakdowns** (not thumbnails with Lamborghinis)
- The good ones: talk risk, show losses, teach mechanics. That's the FORTREX ethos already.

**Telegram** (highest conversion — room owners can mention you directly):
- Channels you're already in: who runs them? Are they educators or just call-droppers? Educators only.
- Search: `NIFTY trading education` · `price action Hindi` · `stock market learning India`
- Check member count on the channel page; 2k–50k members is the sweet spot for a room that actually reads.

**Instagram** (fastest to check, weakest signal — skip if short on time):
- Reels with real chart analysis (not lifestyle flexes); bios linking a Telegram/YouTube = educator with a real room.

**Warm intros beat cold DMs:** your first 25 private invitees are traders — ask each
*"who in your circle teaches or runs a room?"* One warm intro converts better than ten cold DMs.
