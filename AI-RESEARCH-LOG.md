## Standard (applies to every research file)

- **Evidence grading:** A = primary/official source · B = credible secondary ·
  C = community/user evidence · D = hypothesis. D is never converted to fact.
- **Legal items** are always marked `counsel-review-required`. Research is preparation
  for the lawyer, never a substitute.
- Every finding carries: source URL, grade, date. Unverifiable claims say so.
- Files live in `research/universe/`. The command center's earlier research volumes
  (`research/01-06`) remain valid and complementary.

## Research loop (how FORTREX research is done, forever)

Current state → benchmark competitors → academic/industry research → legal check →
identify weaknesses → propose improvements → test → document → implement → measure.
Every major decision asks: should we build it, what is the simplest version that proves
it, how could users abuse it, how does it behave at 1M users, can the founder operate
it without code, and what is the rollback plan.

2. Update the status table (In flight → Landed → Reviewed).
